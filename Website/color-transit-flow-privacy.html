Color Transit Flow

Privacy policy for the offline Android edition, version 1.0.0, package com.jscagames.colortransitflow.

Effective September 21, 2026. This policy describes the supplied offline edition. An edition that enables online advertising will require an updated policy and the applicable in-app privacy controls.

Who we are

Color Transit Flow is provided by JSCA Games. For privacy questions or support, contact jscagames6@gmail.com.

Information used by the game

The game stores your current puzzle, undo history, route progress, earned virtual coins, station appearance and audio/vibration preferences on your device. These records let you resume play. They are not transmitted to JSCA Games.

No accounts, tracking or online ads

This edition does not require an account and does not display ads or offer purchases. Analytics and advertising services are inactive. The Android application has no internet permission and does not send gameplay information or device identifiers to us or advertising providers.

Storage and deletion

Progress remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android backup is disabled for this edition. Clearing data or uninstalling removes your progress and virtual coins. JSCA Games has no server copy to restore.

Optional device features

If you enable vibration in Settings, the game uses the device's vibration feature for feedback. This does not send information to us. Music and sound play locally from bundled recordings.

Support and external links

If you email us, your email address and the information you include are used to respond to your request. Do not include passwords or payment details. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and related issues. Opening this policy or another external link uses your browser; hosting and browser providers may process connection information under their own policies. The game itself does not receive that browsing information.

Audience

The intended store audience is ages 13 and older. The offline game does not ask for a birth date or collect age information.

Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change and update the effective date. Material changes will also be reflected in the app and its store disclosures.
